\section{Manure C and CH\texorpdfstring{$_4$}{4} emissions from livestock manure deposited on pasture}
\label{sec:grazing-manure-ch4}

In Canada, between 16\% (for dairy cattle) and 72\% (for llamas and alpacas) of
manure produced by grazing livestock is managed in pasture, range or paddock
systems (Table~29), where faeces and urine are deposited directly in the field.
Carbon utilization by animals on pasture is a function of the C content of
pasture forage and its digestibility. The entire undigested C content (e.g.,
39\% for beef cattle, Baron et al.\ 2002) is assumed to be returned to the field
and distributed throughout the field by the animal. The amount of C excreted
varies among animal groups and can range between 25 and 60\%
(\url{http://www.soilcc.ca/resources.htm}). The ungrazed portion of the biomass
and the amount of excreted C minus CH$_4$ losses from manure deposited on
pasture is used as an input to the IPCC Tier~2 and ICBM soil C models for the
respective fields/pastures. The amount of N excreted minus direct and indirect N
losses is used as manure inputs to the soil N pool for the respective soil
N$_2$O models.

\subsection{Manure C deposited on pasture}
\label{subsec:grazing-manure-c-deposited}

For livestock on pasture, only faecal C is added to the field (no bedding C).
The amount of C excreted by grazing animals ($C_{\mathrm{excretion}}$) is
estimated using the same approach as for non-grazing livestock --- to estimate
faecal C excretion for each animal group, see Section~4.1.1.1.

\subsection{Direct N\texorpdfstring{$_2$}{2}O emissions from livestock manure deposited on pasture}
\label{subsec:grazing-direct-n2o-1}

Direct daily N$_2$O emissions occur from livestock manure (urine + faeces)
deposited on pasture via combined nitrification and denitrification of N
contained in the manure. The direct N$_2$O emission rate
(kg~N$_2$O~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$) depends on the amount of N excreted by the
animal and default N$_2$O EFs ($\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{direct}}$) from ECCC (2022)
for animals on pasture/range/paddock (Table~36). For grazing livestock in BC,
AB, SK and MB, the direct N$_2$O EF is the same for all soil texture classes and
is based on a study for beef cattle in Western Canada (Lemke et al.\ 2012). For
grazing livestock in ON, QC and the Atlantic provinces, the direct N$_2$O EFs
depend on the soil texture class and are based on a dairy cow study in Eastern
Canada (Rochette et al.\ 2014). The EF is applied to the amount of N excreted by
the animal ($N_{\mathrm{excretion}}$), assuming that 75\% of this is contained in
urine (Rochette et al.\ 2014). For Western Canada, an EF of
0.00043~kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N is applied for all soil texture classes (the EF
for urine is 0.0006 and the EF for dung is 0), while in Eastern Canada a
different EF is applied for each soil texture class:
0.0078~kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N for fine-textured soil,
0.0062~kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N for medium-textured soil and
0.0047~kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N for coarse-textured soil (Rochette et al.\ 2014).

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{For beef cattle, dairy cattle, broilers, layers and turkeys:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture,rate}}
        = N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}} \times \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{direct}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.3.1-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture,rate}}$]
        Direct N$_2$O-N emissions from manure deposited on pasture for beef
        cattle, dairy cattle, broilers, layers and turkeys
        (kg~N$_2$O-N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
    \item[$N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}$]
        Daily N excretion by beef and dairy cattle, broilers, layers and turkeys
        (kg~N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
    \item[$\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{direct}}$]
        Direct N$_2$O-N EF for manure deposited on pasture
        (kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N); for Western Canada (BC, AB, SK, MB),
        0.00043~kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N for all soil texture classes; for
        Eastern Canada (ON, QC, Atlantic provinces) the EF is dependent on soil
        texture: 0.0078 (fine), 0.0062 (medium), 0.0047 (coarse)
        kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N (Table~36)
\end{description}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{For all other livestock:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture,rate}}
        = N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}} \times \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{direct}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.3.1-2}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture,rate}}$]
        Direct N$_2$O-N emissions from manure deposited directly on pasture for
        sheep, swine, poultry (except broilers, layers and turkeys), and other
        livestock (kg~N$_2$O-N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
    \item[$N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}$]
        Daily N excretion (kg~N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$) for sheep
        (Eq.~4.2.1-18), swine (Eq.~4.2.1-24), pullets, broilers and layers
        (Eq.~4.2.1-28), other livestock (Table~42)
    \item[$\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{direct}}$]
        Direct N$_2$O-N EF for urine deposited on pasture
        (kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N); for Western Canada (BC, AB, SK, MB),
        0.00043~kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N for all soil texture classes; for
        Eastern Canada (ON, QC, Atlantic provinces) the EF is dependent on soil
        texture: 0.0078 (fine), 0.0062 (medium), 0.0047 (coarse)
        kg~N$_2$O-N~kg$^{-1}$~N (Table~36)
\end{description}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{For all livestock:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture}}
        = \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture,rate}}
          \times n_{\mathrm{animals}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.3.1-3}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture}}$]
        Direct N$_2$O-N emissions from dung and urine deposited directly on
        pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N~day$^{-1}$), by animal group
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture}}
        = \sum_{\text{all scenario animals}}
          \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.3.1-4}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture}}$]
        Total direct N$_2$O-N emissions from manure for all animals managed on
        pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N), by livestock type
\end{description}

\subsection{NH\texorpdfstring{$_3$}{3} emissions from manure deposited on pasture for beef cattle and dairy cattle}
\label{subsec:grazing-nh3-cattle}

\subsubsection{Urinary nitrogen / total ammoniacal nitrogen (TAN) for beef and dairy cattle (including calves) on pasture}
\label{subsubsec:grazing-tan}

To calculate the amount of TAN excreted in urine by grazing beef and dairy
animals on pasture, use Eq.~4.3.1-1 to Eq.~4.3.1-4.

\subsubsection{Faecal/organic nitrogen for beef and dairy cattle on pasture}
\label{subsubsec:grazing-faecal-n}

For grazing beef and dairy cattle, there is no application of bedding material
and organic N is equal to the amount of N excreted through faeces. To calculate
the amount of N excreted through faeces (faecal N) by grazing beef and dairy
animals on pasture, use Eq.~4.3.1-5 and Eq.~4.3.1-6.

\subsubsection{Ammonia volatilization from manure deposited on pasture for beef and dairy cattle (including calves)}
\label{subsubsec:grazing-nh3-cattle-eq}

Ammonia emissions from grazing beef and dairy cattle on pasture are calculated
on a daily basis.

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{ATA}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
        = \frac{1.041^{\,T}}{1.041^{15}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.1-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=9em, style=sameline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{ATA}_{\mathrm{pasture}}$]
        Ambient temperature-based adjustment used to correct the default NH$_3$
        EF (kg~NH$_3$-N~kg$^{-1}$~TAN, Table~43) for grazed pasture (enclosed,
        open range)
    \item[$T$] Average outdoor daily temperature (\textdegree C)
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}
        = \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{pasture}} \times \mathrm{ATA}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.1-2}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=9em, style=sameline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}$]
        Adjusted NH$_3$ EF for grazed pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N~kg$^{-1}$~TAN)
        $(0 \leq \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}} \leq 1)$
    \item[$\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{pasture}}$]
        Default NH$_3$ EF for grazed pasture (enclosed, open range, Table~43)
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,rate}}
        = \mathrm{TAN}_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}
          \times \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.1-3}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,rate}}$]
        NH$_3$-N emissions from beef and dairy cattle manure deposited directly
        on pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
    \item[$\mathrm{TAN}_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}$]
        Daily TAN excretion rate (kg~TAN~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
        = \mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,rate}}
          \times n_{\mathrm{animals}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.1-4}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}$]
        NH$_3$-N emissions from beef and dairy cattle manure deposited directly
        on pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N), by animal group
    \item[$n_{\mathrm{animals}}$] Number of animals
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
        = \sum_{\text{all scenario animals}} \mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.1-5}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}$]
        Total NH$_3$-N emissions via volatilization from manure for all animals
        managed on pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N), by livestock type
\end{description}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]

\end{description}

\begin{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]

% ·····················································
\subsection{N\texorpdfstring{$_2$}{2}O volatilization from manure deposited on pasture for all livestock}
\label{subsec:grazing-volatilization}

Indirect N$_2$O emissions are calculated on a daily basis for all animal groups.
For beef and dairy cattle, $\mathit{Frac}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}$ values for
each animal group are calculated using Eq.~5.4.3-1; for all other livestock
types on pasture, default IPCC (2019) values are used (Table~36).

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{For beef and dairy cattle (including beef and dairy calves):}

\begin{equation}
    \mathit{Frac}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}
        = \frac{\mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}}{N_{\mathrm{excretion}}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.3-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathit{Frac}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}$]
        Fraction of manure N excreted that is volatilized as NH$_3$ and NO$_x$
        from beef and dairy cattle manure deposited on pasture
        (kg~NH$_3$-N~kg$^{-1}$~N)
    \item[$\mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}$]
        NH$_3$ emissions from beef and dairy cattle managed on pasture
        (kg~NH$_3$-N), by animal group
    \item[$N_{\mathrm{excretion}}$]
        Total amount of N excreted by beef or dairy cattle on pasture
        (kg~N~day$^{-1}$)
\end{description}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{For all animal groups:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture,rate}}
        = N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}
          \times \mathit{Frac}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}
          \times \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.3-2}
\end{equation}

\noindent Derived from IPCC (2019), Eq.~10.26, Eq.~10.28

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture,rate}}$]
        Indirect N$_2$O emissions via volatilization from manure deposited
        directly on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
    \item[$\mathit{Frac}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}$]
        Fraction of manure N excreted that is volatilized as NH$_3$ and NO$_x$
        from manure deposited directly on pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N~kg$^{-1}$~N);
        calculated using Eq.~5.4.3-1 for beef and dairy cattle and derived from
        Table~36 for all other animal groups
    \item[$\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{volatilization}}$]
        EF for volatilization [kg~N$_2$O-N~(kg~NH$_3$-N~volatilized)$^{-1}$]
        (Table~36)
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}
        = \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture,rate}}
          \times n_{\mathrm{animals}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.3-3}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}$]
        Indirect N$_2$O emissions via volatilization from manure deposited
        directly on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N), by animal group
    \item[$n_{\mathrm{animals}}$] Number of animals
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}
        = \sum_{\text{all scenario animals}}
          \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.3-4}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}$]
        Total indirect N$_2$O emissions via volatilization from manure for all
        animals managed on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N), by livestock type
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}$]
        Indirect N$_2$O emissions via volatilization from manure deposited
        directly on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N), by animal group
\end{description}

\subsubsection{Adjustment of NH\texorpdfstring{$_3$}{3} volatilization estimates from manure deposited on pasture following indirect N\texorpdfstring{$_2$}{2}O emissions}
\label{subsubsec:grazing-nh3-adjust}

Adjusted ammonia emissions from manure deposited on pasture are calculated on a
daily basis. Ammonia emissions from manure deposited on pasture need to be
adjusted to avoid double-counting of subsequent indirect N$_2$O-N losses.

\bigskip

\begin{equation}

        = \mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
          - \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.3-5}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}$]
        Adjusted daily NH$_3$-N emissions from manure deposited directly on
        pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N~day$^{-1}$), by animal group
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}
        = \sum_{\text{all scenario animals}}
          \mathrm{NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.3-6}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}}$]
        Adjusted daily NH$_3$-N emissions from manure deposited directly on
        pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N~day$^{-1}$), by livestock type
\end{description}

\subsection{Indirect N losses from manure deposited on pasture via leaching and runoff}
\label{subsec:grazing-leaching}

\noindent\textbf{For all animal groups:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture,rate}}
        = N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}
          \times \mathit{Frac}_{N_{\mathrm{leach}}}
          \times \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{leach}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.4-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture,rate}}$]
        N$_2$O emissions due to leaching and runoff from manure deposited
        directly on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$), by animal
        group
    \item[$N_{\mathrm{excretion,rate}}$]
        N excreted by livestock in manure deposited directly on pasture
        (kg~N~head$^{-1}$~day$^{-1}$)
    \item[$\mathit{Frac}_{N_{\mathrm{leach}}}$]
        Leaching fraction, calculated using Eq.~2.6.6-1
    \item[$\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{leach}}$]
        EF for leaching [kg~N$_2$O-N~(kg~N)$^{-1}$]
\end{description}

\bigskip
\noindent\textbf{Holos V4 uses the following constant value:}

\smallskip
\noindent\begin{tabular}{@{}ll@{}}
    $\mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{leach}}$ & 0.011 (IPCC 2019) \\
\end{tabular}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
        = \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture,rate}}
          \times n_{\mathrm{animals}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.4-2}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}$]
        Indirect N$_2$O-N emissions via leaching and runoff from manure
        deposited directly on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N~day$^{-1}$), by animal group
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
        = \sum_{\text{all scenario animals}}
          \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.4-3}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}$]
        N$_2$O-N leached from manure deposited directly on pasture
        (kg~N$_2$O-N), by livestock type
\end{description}

\noindent To estimate the actual amount of NO$_3$-N leached:

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
        = N_{\mathrm{excretion}}
          \times \mathit{Frac}_{\mathrm{leach}}
          \times \bigl(1 - \mathit{EF}_{\mathrm{leach}}\bigr)
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.4-4}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}$]
        NO$_3$-N leached from manure deposited directly on pasture (kg~NO$_3$-N),
        by animal group
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
        = \sum_{\text{all scenario animals}}
          \mathrm{NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.4-5}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}$]
        NO$_3$-N leached from manure deposited directly on pasture
        (kg~NO$_3$-N), by livestock type
\end{description}

% ·····················································
\subsection{Total indirect N\texorpdfstring{$_2$}{2}O emissions from manure}
\label{subsec:grazing-total-indirect}

\noindent\textbf{For all animal groups:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{indirectpasture}}
        = \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{volatilizationpasture}}
          + \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.5-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{indirectpasture}}$]
        Total indirect N$_2$O emissions from manure deposited on pasture
        (kg~N$_2$O-N~day$^{-1}$), by animal group
\end{description}

% ·····················································
\subsection{Total N\texorpdfstring{$_2$}{2}O emissions from livestock manure deposited on pasture}
\label{subsec:grazing-total-n2o}

\noindent\textbf{For all animal groups:}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}
        = \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{directpasture}}
          + \mathrm{N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{indirectpasture}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.4.6-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}}$]
        Total direct and indirect manure N$_2$O emissions from livestock manure
        deposited on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N), by animal group
\end{description}

\section{Manure C and N deposited on pasture by grazing animals for the ICBM/IPCC Tier 2 and soil N\texorpdfstring{$_2$}{2}O models}
\label{sec:grazing-cn-model}

% ·····················································
\subsection{Carbon}
\label{subsec:grazing-c-model}

Manure C added to soil in grazing field $n$ in year $t$:

\begin{equation}
    C_{\mathrm{model,pasture}(t,n)}
        = \mathrm{Total\,C}_{\mathrm{excretion}(t,n)}
          - \mathrm{Total\,CH_{4,\mathrm{manure}}(t,n)}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.6.1-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$C_{\mathrm{model,pasture}(t,n)}$]
        Total C added to soil from dung and urine deposited directly on pasture
        by grazing animals (kg~C), specific to field $n$ in year $t$; this
        includes C added to soil from all livestock types grazing on field $n$
        in year $t$
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,C}_{\mathrm{excretion}(t,n)}$]
        Total amount of C in dung and urine deposited directly on pasture
        (kg~C), specific to field $n$ in year $t$; sum of manure C excreted by
        all animals grazing on the field in year $t$, by livestock type
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,CH_{4,\mathrm{manure}}(t,n)}$]
        Total CH$_4$ emissions from dung and urine deposited directly on pasture
        (kg~CH$_4$), specific to field $n$ in year $t$; sum of manure CH$_4$
        emissions from the dung and urine deposited on this field by all grazing
        animals in year $t$
\end{description}

% ·····················································
\subsection{Nitrogen}
\label{subsec:grazing-n-model}

Manure N added to soil in grazing field $n$ in year $t$:

\begin{multline}
    N_{\mathrm{model,pasture}(t,n)}
        = \mathrm{Total\,N}_{\mathrm{excretion}(t,n)} \\
          - \Bigl(
              \mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}(t,n)}
              + \mathrm{Total\,NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}(t,n)}
              + \mathrm{Total\,NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}(t,n)}
            \Bigr)
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.6.2-1}
\end{multline}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$N_{\mathrm{model,pasture}(t,n)}$]
        N added to soil from dung and urine deposited directly on pasture by
        grazing animals (kg~N), specific to field $n$ in year $t$; this
        includes N added to soil from all livestock types grazing on field $n$
        in year $t$
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N}_{\mathrm{excretion}(t,n)}$]
        Total amount of N in dung and urine deposited directly on pasture
        (kg~N), specific to field $n$ in year $t$
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N_2O\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture}(t,n)}$]
        Total direct and indirect N$_2$O emissions from dung and urine deposited
        directly on pasture (kg~N$_2$O-N), specific to field $n$ in year $t$;
        sum of all direct and indirect N$_2$O losses from manure deposited on
        this field by all grazing animals in year $t$
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,NH_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{pasture,adju}(t,n)}$]
        Total adjusted NH$_3$ emissions from dung and urine deposited directly
        on pasture (kg~NH$_3$-N), specific to field $n$ in year $t$; sum of
        manure NH$_3$ emissions from dung and urine deposited on this field by
        all grazing animals in year $t$ minus indirect N$_2$O-N emissions via
        volatilization
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,NO_3\text{-}N}_{\mathrm{leachingpasture}(t,n)}$]
        Total leaching NO$_3$-N emissions from dung and urine deposited directly
        on pasture (kg~NO$_3$-N~year$^{-1}$), specific to field $n$ in year
        $t$; sum of manure NO$_3$ emissions from dung and urine deposited on
        this field by all grazing animals in year $t$
\end{description}

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Total\,Volume}_{\mathrm{manurepasture}}
        = \frac{\mathrm{Total\,N}_{\mathrm{excretion}} \times 100}
               {N_{\mathrm{content}} \times 1000}
    \tag{Eq.\ 5.6.2-2}
\end{equation}

\noindent where

\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=12em, style=nextline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,Volume}_{\mathrm{manurepasture}}$]
        Total volume of manure produced by grazing animals on pasture
        (1000~kg~wet~weight), by livestock type
    \item[$\mathrm{Total\,N}_{\mathrm{excretion}}$]
        Total amount of N excreted by grazing animals on pasture
        (kg~N~year$^{-1}$), by livestock type
    \item[$N_{\mathrm{content}}$]
        N content of excreta (urine + faeces; \% wet weight) (Table~6)
\end{description}
