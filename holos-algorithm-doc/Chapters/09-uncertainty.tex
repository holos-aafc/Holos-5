\subsection{Uncertainty estimate for net emissions}
\label{subsec:uncertainty-net}

To determine the overall uncertainty for the estimate of net GHG emissions from
a specified set of farm conditions, the following equation could be used in a
future version of Holos:

\begin{equation}
    \mathrm{Uncertainty} =
    \frac{\left[(A \cdot a)^{2} + (B \cdot b)^{2} + \cdots\right]^{0.5}}
         {\left(A^{2} + B^{2} + \cdots\right)^{0.5}}
    \tag{Eq.\ 9.1.1-1}
\end{equation}

\noindent where
\begin{description}[font=\normalfont, leftmargin=10em, style=sameline, nosep]
    \item[$\mathrm{Uncertainty}$]
        Uncertainty associated with net farm emission estimate
    \item[$A$]
        Emission estimate for each emissions category
        (t~CO$_{2}$ equivalent --- calculated in Chapter~8)
    \item[$a$]
        Uncertainty estimate (Table~58, by uncertainty category associated with
        emission category and relative uncertainty in Table~57)
\end{description}
